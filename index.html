How this course works

Each week we'll have three lectures that culminate in a lab. Our course "week" run Wednesday through Monday — The first lecture of the week will always start on Wednesday, and the lab at the end of each arc on Monday afternoon applies everything you've learned. Especially in the first few weeks, we'll feel this out together as a class.
